# Changelog

## 0.1 (unreleased)
- KIWI NG live ISO with minimal KDE Plasma and a Kitty terminal installer: UEFI and legacy BIOS, LUKS2 + Btrfs, language/keyboard/timezone prompts.
- KDE Discover with RPM and Flatpak support; opt-in multimedia codecs (`ella-codecs`).
- Snapper snapshots around every `dnf` transaction; monthly Btrfs scrub; weekly `fstrim`.
- Themed login screen; `os-release` with Ella name, URLs and image version.
- CI builds the ISO; tagged releases ship checksums.
